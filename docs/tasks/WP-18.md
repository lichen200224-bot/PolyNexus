# WP-18 — Classification, routing, and egress policy

## Scope

Internal deterministic acceptance of D05 classification aggregation,
execution-mode routing, egress decisions, and existing Evidence-based audit
mapping. No new policy Domain entity or persistence schema is introduced.

## Acceptance criteria

- Effective classification is the highest of project/task/artifact inputs.
- `STANDARD`, `LOCAL_PREFERRED`, and `LOCAL_ONLY` are explicit modes.
- Decisions are `ALLOW`, `APPROVAL_REQUIRED`, or `DENY`.
- `LOCAL_ONLY` and `RESTRICTED` deny external egress; untrusted destinations
  deny; sensitive or side-effecting external routes require approval.
- `LOCAL_PREFERRED` selects local when available and otherwise stops at a
  manual/approval boundary; it never silently falls back to cloud.
- Every decision can be mapped to the existing `Evidence` contract with safe,
  bounded metadata and no endpoint, token, credential, or secret value.
