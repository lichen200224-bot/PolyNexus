## Scope and acceptance

- Calculate descriptive completion, failure, evidence-coverage, fallback, and
  explicitly reported manual-operation metrics from existing `Run`/`Evidence`
  records.
- Exclude non-terminal runs from terminal rates and exclude unrelated or
  cross-task evidence even when a run identifier is reused.
- Preserve `None` for unavailable/invalid optional data; never infer success,
  quality, product benefit, or zero usage.
- Remain non-persistent and stable when equivalent records are reloaded.
